ABOUT THE LIBRARY

Learning, research, and community.

The SJCC Library supports the academic community by providing access to information, library resources, services, and welcoming spaces for study and discovery.

OUR ROLE

A library that supports the whole learning experience

The library serves as an academic support center for students, faculty, and staff. Beyond borrowing books, users can seek research assistance, access information resources, study, collaborate, and participate in library programs.

Our goal is to make the library a comfortable and inclusive place where members of the SJCC community feel welcome to learn and connect.
