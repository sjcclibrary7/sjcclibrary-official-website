LIBRARY GUIDELINES

Help keep the library welcoming for everyone.

These are general reminders. Please follow the latest official policies issued by the SJCC Library Department.

01

Respect the learning environment

Keep conversations and device volume at a level appropriate for the library and respect users who are studying.

02

Take care of library materials

Handle books and other resources carefully. Do not write on, tear, damage, or alter library materials.

03

Return materials on time

Observe the applicable borrowing period and return materials according to library instructions.

04

Use reference materials properly

Reference materials such as encyclopedias, yearbooks, annuals, handbooks, almanacs, atlases, periodicals, journals, theses, term papers, and reports may be designated for room use only.

05

Reserve materials

Reserve materials are subject to room-use and borrowing arrangements established by the library. Ask staff when you are unsure.

06

Respect other users' belongings

Keep personal belongings secure and do not leave valuables unattended. Report lost or found items to library staff.

07

Use library equipment responsibly

Follow staff instructions when using computers, printing, photocopying, and other library facilities.

08

Ask for assistance

Library staff are available to help you find resources, understand procedures, and use library services.
